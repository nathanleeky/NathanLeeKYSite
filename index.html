The Deliverables

Case studies in enterprise strategy

1
Ongoing

Institutional Territory Management

Coverage lived in spreadsheets, decks and people's heads, and each business line did it differently. I created the strategy, the program and the tool. It is a governed system of record for client ownership, territory assignments and coverage accountability. Coverage models are built by geography, industry and relationship manager, with exception management for shared coverage, regional splits and industry overrides. Users can find the coverage owner, relationship managers and product specialists for a client, and reporting shows coverage gaps and ownership conflicts. Built with Power Apps, SharePoint and Power Automate.

500+ users on one governed record of who covers which client

Ongoing

Executive Competitor Insights

I lead a quarterly competitor analysis program that synthesizes earnings reports, financial performance data, market signals, and strategic moves from competitor banks into clear executive narratives. The insights focus on implications, risks, and options rather than raw data, with structured recommendations that help leaders prioritize responses, refine positioning, and align on capital allocation. Over time, this has become a repeatable decision support capability that strengthens strategic clarity and execution.

Executive-level deck delivered quarterly to CEO and Board

The Thinking

How I approach the work

Name the owner first

When nobody owns a client or a decision, nothing moves. I start by deciding who owns what.

A plan needs a routine

A strategy with no owner, no approval path and no regular check-in is just a slide deck.

Make the right thing the easy thing

A tool works when the behavior you want is also the simplest one.

People use what they trust

I build with the people who will use it. That is how a tool gets adopted.
