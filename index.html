The Thinking

A.I. Philosophy

How I think about AI solving real selling challenges.

AI automates the administrative, not the relational

The best use of AI in sales is automating the work that keeps salespeople from selling — not the prospecting call, but the CRM data entry.

Enablement is about eliminating friction

Every tool should have a clear answer to "what problem does this solve for the person using it?" Real enablement makes their job easier.

Systems scale consistency, not shortcuts

Good systems are invisible. They make it easier to do the right thing than to cut corners, and they distribute expertise across a team.

Adoption requires trust

People adopt tools when they trust leadership believes in it, it will help them, and they were part of shaping it.
